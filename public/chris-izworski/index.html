Chris Izworski

The angler and builder behind Michigan Trout Report, writing from Bay City on the Saginaw Bay watershed.

Chris Izworski is the creator of Michigan Trout Report, a Michigan fishing publication and set of tools for deciding where and when to fish. Based in Bay City on Saginaw Bay, he brings his interest in trout fishing together with software that makes stream conditions easier to compare before a trip.

What Chris builds and writes here

The stream conditions report brings USGS gauge readings and National Weather Service forecasts into one view, with river comparisons, flow, available water temperatures, and fly and lure recommendations. The stream map gives the same trip a geographic starting point. The salmon run tracker covers another Michigan fishing decision: which rivers to watch during the run.

His guide, How to Read Michigan Trout Water, explains how to weigh regulations, temperature, streamflow, weather and river character together. It is a reading framework to use alongside the report, rather than a promise that a rating predicts your catch.

Measurements, interpretation and missing data

Flow and available water temperatures originate with USGS gauges; forecasts originate with the National Weather Service. The software compares flow with the gauge’s published daily historical statistics when available. The length of that record depends on the gauge; it is not a fixed thirty-year measurement for every river.

Fishing ratings and tackle suggestions are the report’s interpretation of those inputs, not USGS or NWS recommendations. A stream may lack a working temperature sensor or a usable flow reading. Check the source and conditions at the river before relying on a comparison, and verify current fishing rules with the Michigan Department of Natural Resources.

Background

Chris Izworski spent more than a decade running 911 centers in Michigan, as Director of Bay County 911 and later Executive Director of Saginaw County 911, before moving into public safety technology. That operational background, building systems that have to be accurate because people rely on them under pressure, is the same instinct that runs through Michigan Trout Report and the rest of his work. His writing on emergency communications and practical AI has appeared in NENA's national publication, The Call. The full professional record lives at chrisizworski.com.

The trout fishing is the older thread. Chris grew up fishing Michigan water and has paddled wilderness canoe routes in Ontario for years, which is the calibration that informs how he writes about Michigan rivers: clear-eyed about what the state offers, which is a great deal, and honest about what it does not.
